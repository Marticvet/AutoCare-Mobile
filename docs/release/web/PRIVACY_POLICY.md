## Data we process

Depending on the features you choose to use, we process:

- Account and profile data, such as your email address, account identifier, display name, phone number, and profile image.
- Vehicle and activity data, including vehicle details, mileage, expenses, fuel or charging records, maintenance, reminders, trips, budgets, checklists, and reports.
- Documents and images you choose to upload, such as receipts or vehicle documents.
- Location data only when you actively use a location-related feature, such as nearby services or attaching a location to an expense. The app requests foreground location permission only.
- Subscription and entitlement information, such as store product, purchase status, renewal status, and anonymous/store customer identifiers needed to provide paid access.
- Technical information needed to operate and secure the Service, including authentication/session data, IP/network information processed by service providers, and limited server error logs.

We do not sell personal data or use it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

## Why we use data

We use data to provide the Service; synchronize it across your devices; maintain account security; process subscriptions and restore purchases; respond to support requests; prevent abuse; meet legal obligations; and improve reliability.

Where applicable, our legal bases are performance of a contract, legitimate interests in operating and protecting the Service, consent (for permissions or optional processing), and legal obligations.

## Service providers and sharing

We use service providers to operate the Service. Current intended providers include:

- Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and Edge Functions.
- PowerSync for secure synchronization of app data.
- RevenueCat and Apple/Google billing services for subscription management and purchase entitlement information.
- Google Maps/Places for a user-requested nearby-services or address-search feature.
- [[document OCR provider, if enabled]] for optional document text extraction.

We share data only as needed to provide the Service, comply with law, protect rights and safety, or complete a corporate transaction. Shared-garage data is visible to people you invite or authorize under the feature’s access controls.

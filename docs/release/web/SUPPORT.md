## Getting started

1. Create an account and add your first vehicle.
2. Add fuel, service, insurance, or general expenses from the relevant section.
3. Set reminders for maintenance or renewal dates.
4. Allow notifications if you want scheduled reminder alerts.
5. Sign in on another device to synchronize your authorized account data.

## Subscription help

Use **Subscription** in the app to view your plan, restore purchases, or manage a subscription. Apple and Google process billing and refunds under their own store policies. Include your platform, product name, and purchase date when contacting support; never send your password or full payment details.

## Sync and offline use

AutoCare Hub saves changes locally first and synchronizes them when a network connection is available. If changes are pending, keep the app open and connected, then use the sync retry control in Settings. Do not uninstall the app before pending changes are synchronized.

## Account, data export, and deletion

Settings includes options to export account data and permanently delete an account. Account deletion is irreversible after processing. If you cannot access your account, contact support from the account’s registered email address.

## Troubleshooting

- Update to the latest app version.
- Check your connection and sign in again if prompted.
- For nearby-services search, confirm foreground location permission is allowed.
- For reminder alerts, allow notifications in device settings.
- If a purchase is not recognized, use **Restore purchases** before contacting support.

## What to include in a support request

Send the app version, device model, operating-system version, a description of the issue, and non-sensitive screenshots if useful. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, payment-card data, or government identifiers.
